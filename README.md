# Simple Tophats

![Logo](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/hDcQ3Xm1/images/7709d6cb41dd759b1e2025bf38967ea2e130c199.png)

Simple Tophats

A datapack + resource pack pair that adds vanilla compatible tophats! They can be crafted with wool slabs and wool blocks, and create dapper looking tophats.

![Recipe](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/hDcQ3Xm1/images/f6121499149f946c42023d2a770bbe0418ee17fc.png)

Wear your tophat! Impress your friends! Decorate your foxes, zombies and skeletons!
